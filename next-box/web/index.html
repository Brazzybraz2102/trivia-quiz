      Snap your page

      Mark the label ✓ done, → move, ✗ drop, then take a photo. In a journal, get both pages in the photo: your handwritten notes are read too and saved to your Journal. Drops and anything uncertain wait for you here. Your photo is read by Claude (Anthropic).

      Your journal

      Every page you snap is kept here with its photo, so a lost notebook is never a lost record. Only you can read your journal.

      Look back on my week

      A short, kind note about your last 7 days: what went well, something that kept sliding, and one tiny idea for next week.

      Ask your Next Box owner to add an AI key.

      This sends your week's task names to Anthropic's AI to write the note. Nothing is kept by Next Box unless you save it.

      Slugs: things that keep sliding

      No judgment. A task that keeps moving is usually too big or unclear. Make it smaller, print it alone, or let it go.
