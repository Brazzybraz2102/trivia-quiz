Next Box

Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · Plain language, no fine print.

The short version. Next Box keeps what it needs to run your to-do list and labels, on a computer run by Braznarkel. There are no ads, no trackers and nothing is sold. You can download everything or delete your account any time from your Profile.

Who runs this

This Next Box is run by Braznarkel as an invite-only beta. Questions: ask the person who invited you.

What Next Box keeps

  • Your account: username, optional email and display name, your settings. Your password is stored only as a scrambled one-way hash; nobody can read it.
  • Your list: tasks, tiny steps and tags you add to Next Box's own list.
  • What you print: each ticket's text and image, so a photo of it can be matched back to your tasks.
  • Read-backs: what was read from your photo (which boxes you marked). The photo itself is not kept.
  • Sign-ins and activity: your signed-in devices (device type and network address) and a log of actions, used to fix problems.
  • Connections: if you link a to-do app or calendar, its access key, locked with encryption. Tasks and events from linked apps are read when needed and not stored, except what ends up on a ticket you print.
  • Feedback: shown to everyone without your name. Who sent what is kept separately and only the admin can look it up.

Who can see it

You see your own things. The admin of this Next Box can see how you use it: your printed tickets, read-backs, settings, activity, devices and how many tasks are on your list (not what they say). You were shown this when you first signed in. Your password and linked-app keys are never visible to anyone.

Services Next Box uses

  • Cloudflare carries traffic between your phone and Next Box (secure https).
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads the marks on a photo when you use photo read-back. The photo is sent for that one job.
  • Apps you choose to link (for example Todoist, Google, Microsoft, Notion): Next Box talks to them only for you.

Cookies

One cookie, to keep you signed in. No tracking or advertising cookies, and no analytics in the app.

How long things are kept

Printed tickets and read-backs are deleted after 90 days. The activity log is deleted after 180 days. Your account and list stay until you delete them.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Profile → Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete my account. This removes your account, list, tags, tickets and history.
  • Unlink an app or calendar any time in Settings.

Kids

Next Box isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, you'll be told in the app before it applies to you.
