Next Box

Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · Plain language, no fine print.

The short version. Next Box keeps what it needs to run your to-do list and labels, on a computer run by Braznarkel. There are no ads, no trackers and nothing is sold. You can download everything or delete your account any time from your Profile.

Who runs this

This Next Box is run by Braznarkel as an invite-only beta. Questions: ask the person who invited you.

What Next Box keeps

  • Your account: username, optional email and display name, your settings. Your password is stored only as a scrambled one-way hash; nobody can read it.
  • Your list: tasks, tiny steps and tags you add to Next Box's own list.
  • What you print: each ticket's text and image, so a photo of it can be matched back to your tasks.
  • Read-backs: which boxes you marked on a photo. With Journal mode on (it's on unless you turn it off), the photo and the handwriting read from it are kept as a page in your private Journal. With it off, a photo is kept only when it has handwriting (notes or a win), as that day's journal page. Delete pages any time in Journal.
  • Your journal: page photos, notes you type or that were read from your handwriting, and your wins (what you finished each day; a linked app's task names aren't kept). Only you can see them: there's no admin view, and admin tools hide them. They stay until you delete the page or your account (the 90-day limit below doesn't apply).
  • Sign-ins and activity: your signed-in devices (device type and network address) and a log of actions, used to fix problems.
  • Connections: if you link a to-do app or calendar, its access key, locked with encryption. Tasks and events from linked apps are read when needed and not stored, except what ends up on a ticket you print.
  • Feedback: shown to everyone without your name. Who sent what is kept separately and only the admin can look it up.

Who can see it

You see your own things. The admin of this Next Box can see how you use it: your printed tickets, read-backs, settings, activity, devices and how many tasks are on your list (not what they say). You were shown this when you first signed in. Your journal, your password and linked-app keys are never visible to anyone else.

Services Next Box uses

  • carries traffic between your phone and Next Box (secure https).
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads the marks and transcribes your handwritten notes on a photo when you use Snap. The photo is sent for that one job, with the marks you use (each symbol, what Next Box does with it, and your own words for what it means, from Settings → How you mark your list) so it knows how to read them. The AI helpers use it too, only when you tap them: Break it down sends that task's name, how long it takes, its day, its steps and anything you type in “What makes it hard?”, and Look back on my week sends your week's task names (and your journal notes only if you tick them). Next Box keeps nothing from them unless you save it. You can turn them off in Settings → Focus.
  • Apps you choose to link (for example Todoist, Google, Microsoft, Notion): Next Box talks to them only for you.

Cookies

One cookie, to keep you signed in. No tracking or advertising cookies, and no analytics in the app.

How long things are kept

Printed tickets and read-backs are deleted after 90 days. The activity log is deleted after 180 days. Your account, list and journal stay until you delete them.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Profile → Download my data. One .zip file with your list, settings, journal pages and their photos, and ticket images.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete my account. This removes your account, list, tags, journal pages and photos, tickets and history.
  • Delete a journal page any time in Journal. Turn off Journal mode in Settings → Focus to keep only photos with handwriting.
  • Unlink an app or calendar any time in Settings.

Kids

Next Box isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, you'll be told in the app before it applies to you.
