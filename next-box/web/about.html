About Next Box

Your to-do list, printed. Next Box turns today's tasks into a short ticket and stickers you put where the task happens. Tick them off, snap a photo, and your list updates. Snap your paper journal too: your handwriting is kept in a private Journal that only you can read. Built for brains that forget what they can't see.
