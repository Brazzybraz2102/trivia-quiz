Next Box quick start

Your to-do list, on paper you can stick where things happen. Ten minutes from here to your first sticker.

1Join

You need an invite. Tap the link you were sent, or open Next Box, tap Have an invite code? Join and type the code.

Pick a username (lowercase, like sam) and a password of 8 or more characters. Each code works once.

The first time you sign in, you'll see what the admin can see about how you use Next Box. Tap I understand and agree.

Open Next Box

2Put it on your home screen

  • iPhone: in Safari tap Share (the square with the arrow) → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  • Computer: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right of the address bar. No icon? A bookmark works too.

Now it opens like an app, and it's always the newest version. There's nothing to update.

On the home-network address (the one starting http://) this makes a shortcut; the https:// address makes a real app.

3Empty your head

On My list, type everything on your mind into Brain dump, one thing per line, and tap Add to my list. Don't sort it.

Optional shortcuts on any line:

#erranda tag (tags have colors)
!urgent
today tomorrow friwhen it's due
3pma time
~15mhow long it'll take
every dayrepeats (also every week, every month)
Example: Pay water bill #errand ! tomorrow ~10m

Tap Edit on a task to add tiny steps. Make the first one take two minutes, like "find the bill".

4Just one thing

The top of My list shows the one task to do now, with its next tiny step.

  • Done ✓: that counts.
  • Print just this: a card with just that task and its steps.
  • Not now: shows the next one.

5Stickers and tear-off strips

Tick tasks with the boxes on the left (or tick none to use today's), then tap:

  • Stickers: one small label per task, to stick on the bill, the door, the car keys.
  • Tear-off strips: one label you cut into strips.

Each tag has a color. On plain white labels, colors print as patterns (red is solid black, yellow is dots, blue is stripes), so you can still tell them apart. Change tags in Settings → Tags & colors.

Keep Preview only ticked (top of the page) to try things without printing.

6Your daily ticket

Print → Today prints today's list: your calendar at the top, then up to 5 tasks with the next tiny step under each, with the rest listed small. It ends with "Yesterday you finished 4. ✓"

Change any of it in Settings → Focus & ADHD helpers.

7Tick, snap, done

Mark a printed ticket or sticker with a pen: ✓ done, → move to tomorrow, ✗ drop. Then open Snap and take a photo. Your list updates.

Anything it isn't sure about, and every delete, waits for you to tap Confirm.

Using a paper journal? Stick today's label on the left page, work your list, and write notes about your day on the right page. At night, snap both pages in one photo. Your marks update your list, your handwriting is typed out, and the page is kept in your Journal with its photo, so losing the notebook never means losing your days. The Journal also shows your wins each week and any slugs: tasks that keep sliding to another day, so you can make them smaller or let them go. Only you can read your journal.

8Your apps and calendars (optional)

Next Box has its own list, so you don't need anything else. If you already use an app, link it in Settings and its tasks show up next to yours (they stay in that app).

WorksTodoist, Notion, Asana, monday.com, Nextcloud/Fastmail task lists, and Google Tasks, Microsoft To Do and TickTick once your admin turns them on
CalendarsGoogle, Outlook, iCloud, Proton, Yahoo, or any calendar with a subscription (.ics) link
Can'tApple Reminders, Things, Any.do, Google Keep. These apps don't let other apps in. Use My list instead.

9Printing: label printer or any printer

No label printer? Use any printer. After you make stickers, a list or a card, tap Print on paper. It opens a page you print the normal way: Share → Print on iPhone, ⋮ → Print on Android, or Ctrl+P on a computer.

  • Stickers come out real size in a grid with cut lines. Use full-sheet sticker paper (sold as "full sheet labels", like Avery 5165 / L7167), then cut and stick.
  • Lists and cards print on plain paper.
  • Pick your paper size (Letter, A4, A5 or Half letter) in Settings → Printing.

Label printer: whoever runs your household adds it in Admin → Printers (Brother QL, most thermal label and receipt printers). Then tickets print straight to it.

10Help and feedback

Tap Send feedback (on a phone, it's in the menu under your initial at the top right). It's shared with everyone but never with your name. Something confusing is the most useful thing you can report. Everyone's feedback is on the Feedback board.

The Get started checklist at the top of My list walks you through all of this. Bring it back any time in Settings → Help.
